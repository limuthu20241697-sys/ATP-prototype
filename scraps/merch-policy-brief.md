## Why this is needed

We are launching an online store to sell Open Air Colombo merchandise (flag scarfs, paper fans, silicone wristbands, collector boxes). The store is built and the site work is done.

Before we can accept a single payment, our payment gateway (**OnePay**) runs a bank compliance review. That review **will reject us** unless our policy pages are published and live on the store, with a real physical address and a working phone number. Shopify separately marks **Contact information** as required before the store can operate.

The review takes **up to 3 working days**, and the clock does not start until these pages exist. This document is therefore on the critical path.

**What we need from you:** the decisions and legal/business content below. The web team will handle all formatting, publishing and visual design once you return this.
